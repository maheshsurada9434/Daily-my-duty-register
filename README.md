## What it does

- **Setup in one step:** enter your department name, your usual duty timing and the nature of your duty.
- **Daily entry in a few taps:**
  - date, department, duty timing (From/To) and nature of duty, plus place and remarks
  - duty hours are counted for you, including night duties that end the next day
  - also records **leave** (CL, EL, medical and others), **holidays** and **weekly off**
- **Fast filling:**
  - "Same as last duty" button
  - repeat an entry until a chosen date (skips Sundays if you want)
  - "Mark Sundays as weekly off" fills them in one go
- **Monthly duty statement:**
  - calendar view and day-by-day table
  - totals for duty days, duty hours, extra hours, night duties, leave and holidays
  - nature-of-duty, department and leave summaries
- **Yearly duty statement:** month-by-month table with totals and a chart.
- **Custom date statement:** this week, last month, last 3 months, the financial year, or any dates you choose.
- **Print / Save as PDF:** an official-style A4 statement with your details, a certification line, and signature spaces for you and the officer in charge.
- **Excel (CSV) export, share summary, backup and restore.**
- **Works offline and installs like an app:**
  - iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
  - Android: Chrome → Install app
- **Private:** your register is stored only on your device. There is no login and no server.

## Settings

In Settings you can:

- add more departments, duty timings and kinds of duty
- choose your defaults (★)
- set your normal hours per day, which is used to count extra hours
- fill in your name, designation, employee ID and office for the printed statement

## Run it yourself

It is one HTML file with no build step. Open `index.html`, or host the folder anywhere. GitHub Pages publishes it automatically through `.github/workflows/pages.yml`.
